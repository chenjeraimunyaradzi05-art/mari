## 3. AI-POWERED MONETIZATION ENGINE

### 3.1 Dynamic Pricing & Revenue Optimization

#### AI Pricing Models

**Programmatic CPM Auctions (Real-Time Bidding)**
- **Predictive Quality Score**: ML model predicts CTR per impression (0-100 score)
- **Quality-Adjusted CPM**: Higher quality impressions command 2-5x CPM multiplier
  - Example: Base CPM AU$15 × quality factor 1.8 = effective CPM AU$27
- **Demand Forecasting**: Predict peak demand hours, auto-increase floor price (e.g., peak hours +20% floor)
- **Competitor Pricing**: Monitor competitor CPMs in real-time, adjust platform pricing 5-10% above to capture premium demand

**Dynamic CPA Pricing (Lead Generation)**
- **Cost Per Enquiry (CPE)** base: AU$25 (industry benchmark)
- **Adjustment Factors** (ML-learned):
  - Vertical adjustment: Yachting (+40% premium), Corporate (-10% discount)
  - Season adjustment: Peak hiring season (+25%), off-season (-15%)
  - Org reputation: High-quality employers (-15% to attract), new employers (+20% to incentivize)
  - Lead quality: Hot leads (score >75) sell at +50% CPE, cold leads (score <25) at -40%

**Premium Tier Pricing (Memberships & Subscriptions)**
- **Price Optimization**: A/B test 3-5 price points, measure elasticity (demand vs. price)
- **Cohort Pricing**: Trial users offered 50% discount (convert to paid), returning users at 10% premium
- **Bundle Discounts**: 3-month plan at 15% discount, annual plan at 25% discount (increase LTV)
- **Win-Back Pricing**: Churned users offered 40% discount (reactivation campaign)

### 3.2 Recommendation Engine for Ad Placement

#### Intelligent Targeting

**User Propensity Models** (ML Classification)
- **Job Interest Propensity**: Predict likelihood user is job-seeking (scale 0-1)
  - Features: profile completeness, job search activity, CV uploads, saved jobs, resume edits
  - Usage: Show job ads to users with score >0.6
  
- **Course Interest Propensity**: Predict likelihood user is seeking training
  - Features: skill gaps, upskilling interest signals, career stage, previous course views
  - Usage: Show course ads to users with score >0.7

- **Spending Power Propensity**: Predict likelihood user can afford premium services
  - Features: job title seniority, income data (optional), membership history
  - Usage: Price premium membership higher for high-score users (price discrimination)

- **Engagement Propensity**: Predict likelihood user will engage with ads (watch video, click CTA)
  - Features: historical CTR, engagement rate, content type preference, time-on-app
  - Usage: Show ads to users with score >0.5 (reduce ad waste)

**Contextual Relevance Scoring**
- **Content Matching**: User viewing apprenticeship content → show apprenticeship ads
- **Time-of-Day Matching**: User browsing jobs at 8am (working hours) → likely employed, show executive jobs
- **Previous Searches**: User searched "hospitality jobs Sydney" → show hotel ads in Sydney
- **Social Proof**: User has 50+ followers (influencer proxy) → show premium org ads

#### Real-Time Bidding Signals
- **Impression Value Score** (0-100): Combines propensity + relevance + recency
  - Score >80: "Premium impression" (suggest high bid)
  - Score 40-80: "Standard impression" (standard bid)
  - Score <40: "Low-value impression" (reduce bid or skip)

- **Optimal Ad Format Recommender**: For each impression, ML predicts best format
  - Example: Young user, mobile device, high engagement → recommend video ad (CTR +40%)
  - Example: Senior user, desktop, business context → recommend lead-gen form (conversion +25%)

- **User Query**: "I want flexible work as a medical administrator, part-time, Sydney, no weekends"

- **Embedding Model**: Embed jobs + candidate profiles as high-dimensional vectors
- **Similarity Matching**: Find jobs semantically similar to user's past experience
- **Example**: User previously worked "Customer Support Rep" → recommend "Community Manager", "Client Success Manager" (similar work, different titles)
- **Cold Start**: New users get 10 recommendations on day 1 (based on profile quiz)

- **Filters**:
  - Industry (Healthcare, Tech, Education, etc.)
  - Role Type (Full-time, Part-time, Contract, Casual)
  - Location (Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane, etc.)

  - Company Size (Startup <50, SME 50-500, Enterprise >500)
  - Work Environment (Remote, Hybrid, On-site)
  - Experience Required (Entry-level, Mid-level, Senior)
- **Facet Counts**: Show job count per facet (e.g., "Full-time: 342 jobs, Part-time: 128 jobs")
