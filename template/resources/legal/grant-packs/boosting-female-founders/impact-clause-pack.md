### Gender impact clause

The Company commits to advancing gender equality through measurable KPIs covering leadership representation, pay equity reviews, and supplier diversity. The board will table a quarterly gender impact scorecard summarising:

- Percentage of leadership roles held by women and non-binary leaders
- Pay parity variance across each level (+/- 3%)
- Dollars invested into female-led supply partners

Failure to table the scorecard within 30 days of quarter-end triggers an automatic governance review and remedial action plan.

### Community reporting covenant

The Company will publish an annual public impact snapshot that aligns to the Boosting Female Founders reporting pack. Minimum inclusions:

1. Headline impact statement (≤ 200 words)
2. Three outcome metrics tied to STEM participation, export, or job creation
3. Description of how grant funds were allocated against eligible cost buckets

The board authorises the Impact & Inclusion sub-committee to sign off the snapshot and notify funders once published.
