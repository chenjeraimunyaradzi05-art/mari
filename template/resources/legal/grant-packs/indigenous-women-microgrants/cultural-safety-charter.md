### Cultural safety charter (excerpt)

- **Free, prior, informed consent** – No public storytelling, imagery, or data is released without documented consent from the knowledge holders.
- **Language priority** – Where possible, collateral is co-developed in local language first, with English translations appended. Budget has a protected line item for translation and community review fees.
- **Cultural IP protection** – Any technology, curriculum, or materials derived from First Nations knowledge will be licensed under a custodial agreement that remains owned by the community.
- **Wellbeing guardrails** – Travel, participation, and research activities include wellbeing budgets covering childcare, safe travel, and mental health support following cultural load periods.
