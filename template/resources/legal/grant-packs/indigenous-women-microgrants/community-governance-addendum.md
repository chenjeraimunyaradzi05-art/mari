### Community governance addendum

1. **Elders Advisory Circle** – The Company recognises the authority of the nominated Elders Advisory Circle. Any change to objectives, branding, or community IP must be tabled at least 28 days prior for cultural approval. Minutes from Elders deliberations are annexed to each grant acquittal.
2. **Decision-making quorum** – For matters affecting cultural IP, quorum requires at least one Elder, one founder, and one community member. If quorum is not met, the motion is deferred until a community yarning session can be held.
3. **Benefit-sharing register** – The Company maintains a register of social benefit allocations (micro-grants, scholarships, cultural engagement fees). Updates are published quarterly for transparency.

### Dispute pathway

- Stage 1: Yarning circle facilitated by a neutral First Nations mediator within 10 business days.
- Stage 2: Binding recommendation from Elders Advisory Circle.
- Stage 3: If unresolved, the parties may enter into mediation under the Indigenous Mediation Panel before escalating to courts.
