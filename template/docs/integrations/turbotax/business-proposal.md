# Business Proposal — TurboTax Integration (ATHENA)

## Executive Summary
ATHENA proposes a strategic integration with TurboTax (Intuit) to offer tax preparation, projections, and optimization features across ATHENA’s Life Operating System for Women. The integration strengthens ATHENA’s Finance Economic Engine, Formation Studio, Housing Engine, Life Pathway Engine and AI services — creating a sticky, end-to-end financial experience.

## Proposed Business Models
- Freemium: Basic tax projections included, advanced features behind paywall
- Subscription add-on: Monthly or annual add-on for full tax preparation and live advisory
- Per-filing fee: Charge per return filed (like TurboTax) or offer co-branded filings
- Referral / affiliate: Offer TurboTax as referral while capturing upstream ATHENA data
- Premium advisory services: Charge for personalized tax advisory delivered by certified agents or AI-enhanced advisors

## Partnership Approaches
1. API integration (recommended): Work with Intuit to use TurboTax APIs to provide in-app tax calculation and filing. Quick to value and maintain user experience within ATHENA.
2. White-label: License TurboTax engines and brand it as "ATHENA Tax" — greater control and deeper integration, but more cost and timeline.
3. Referral / partnership: Lowest lift to test demand — link to TurboTax and capture referral commissions.

## Commercial asks from Intuit (recommended to negotiate)
- Sandbox and Developer API access
- Developer licensing and rate allocations
- Revenue share structure for referral or co-branded filings
- White-label licensing terms for productized ATHENA Tax (optional)

## Cost & Timeline estimates
- Discovery & legal contracting: 1–3 months
- Pilot (tax projections): 3–6 months
- E-file + deeper coverage: additional 3–6 months
- White-label (if chosen) + global localization: 6–18 months

## Key Risks & Mitigations
- Regulatory liability for tax advice: use disclaimers, partner with licensed advisors and limit advisory scope to algorithmic recommendations unless certified advisors are used
- Data sensitivity: apply strict encryption and consent flows, use Intuit-approved secure channels
- User experience complexity: start with projections and recommendations, avoid forcing users into filing in-phase 1

## Recommendation & Next Steps
1. Secure sandbox access and confirm API features with Intuit.
2. Run a small closed pilot with 50–200 users for a single persona (e.g., Business Owners or Members with side income)
3. Deliver roadmap items (technical POC, UX mockups, compliance review)
