# Slide 1 — Title
ATHENA × TurboTax — Empowering women to build wealth, with tax-aware planning built-in

# Slide 2 — Problem
- Women entrepreneurs and wealth-builders use fragmented tools for accounting, tax, and business formation.
- Planning vs. after-tax outcomes are disconnected—this creates poor business decisions and missed optimization.

# Slide 3 — Opportunity
- Combine ATHENA’s lifecycle platform (Formation Studio, Finance Engines, Life Pathways) with TurboTax’s tax engine to deliver a unique, holistic product.
- Cross-sell opportunities and sticky annual tax events increase retention and monetization.

# Slide 4 — Proposed Integration (MVP)
- API Integration for tax projections
- Start with W2/1099, self-employed, and rental property projections
- Surface tax projections in Formation Studio and Finance Dashboard

# Slide 5 — Go-to-market
- Pilot with a subset of business-builders & formation users
- Co-marketing campaigns & shared landing pages
- Revenue options: referral, co-branded premium, subscription add-on

# Slide 6 — Security & Privacy
- Explicit opt-in for data sharing
- SOC2 and PII controls; collaborate on contract & data handling

# Slide 7 — Timeline & Cost
- Pilot: 2–3 months
- Production API integration: 6–12 months
- Costs: initial dev + ongoing licensing depending on partnership model

# Slide 8 — Ask from TurboTax
- Sandbox API access & partner engineering contact
- High-level commercial term guide (referral vs licensing)
- Quick legal NDA to start discovery
