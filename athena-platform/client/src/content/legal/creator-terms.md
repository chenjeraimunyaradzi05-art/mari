# Creator Terms Addendum

This addendum is part of the [ATHENA Terms of Service](/terms). Section 5 of the Terms says that to be paid as a creator you must accept it, and this is the text you are accepting. Where it says what happens to money, it says what the platform does today, nothing more.

---

## 1. Who can be paid as a creator

To turn on creator mode and to be paid, you must:

- Be at least 18 years old. Your date of birth on your account is checked on the server each time you turn on creator mode, connect payouts, or ask for a payout.
- Hold an account in good standing. An account that is suspended or banned cannot turn on creator mode, cannot be sent gifts, and cannot withdraw.
- Complete the identity checks that Stripe, our payments provider, asks for when you connect payouts. You are paid only once Stripe has verified your account and switched payouts on for it.
- Accept this addendum, in the version shown on this page. If it is rewritten, you are asked to accept the new version before your next withdrawal, and your earnings are untouched in the meantime.

## 2. What you earn

- Members send you gifts with points they have bought in Australian dollars. You keep {{price.creatorShareRange}} of the value of each gift, depending on your creator tier, and the rest is ATHENA's fee ({{price.platformFeeRange}}). Your tier, and the share at each tier, are shown on your creator dashboard and on the [fees page](/fees).
- Card processing is covered by ATHENA's share. No separate processing fee is taken from what you keep, and ATHENA takes no fee when you withdraw.
- Your share is worked out when the gift is sent, at the tier you hold at that moment.

## 3. Payouts

- The minimum payout is {{price.minimumPayout}}. There is no other threshold and no waiting period of ours: once your balance has reached the minimum, you can ask for it from your creator dashboard. Nothing is paid out automatically.
- Payouts are made in Australian dollars, through Stripe Connect, to your own Stripe account. Stripe then pays your bank on the schedule it sets for the account, under its own terms.
- A balance below the minimum carries over until it reaches it.

## 4. Refunds, chargebacks and pauses

- If a member's purchase of gift points is refunded or disputed with their bank, the points that purchase bought are taken back, and the share of any gift paid from them may be taken back from your balance. Nothing is taken from you without a member of ATHENA's team deciding it.
- While a card payment connected to gifts you were sent is being looked at, we may pause your withdrawals. Your balance stays yours and keeps growing, and we write to you when withdrawals are open again.

## 5. Tax

- You are responsible for your own tax. ATHENA does not withhold tax from what you earn and does not give tax advice.
- Your earnings statement, available from your dashboard, is a record of what ATHENA released to you. It is not a tax invoice and not a payment summary from an employer. Ask a registered tax agent or the ATO how to report this income.

## 6. Your content and conduct

- The Terms of Service and the [Community Guidelines](/help/community-guidelines) apply to everything you publish and stream as a creator.
- If your account is suspended under the Terms, gifts to you stop and so do withdrawals, until the suspension is lifted.

## 7. Changes

- When this addendum changes, the version on this page changes with it. Nobody is treated as having agreed to text they never saw: a creator who accepted an earlier version is asked again before her next withdrawal.
