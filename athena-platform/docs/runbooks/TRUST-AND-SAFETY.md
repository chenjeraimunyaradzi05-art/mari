# Trust & Safety: the serious reports

**Read when a report comes in for an intimate image shared without consent, for a threat to hurt someone, or for anything else the alert marks `[CRITICAL]`.** For a person who may be under 18, read `UNDER-AGE-ACCOUNT.md` instead. For a woman who may hurt herself, the wellness forum and the report queue both raise it; the numbers to give her are on the Emergency help button.

> **Status: needs the owner's and counsel's sign-off before public launch.** The steps below describe what the platform does and what a person on the safety team does next. They are written to be safe by default. Nothing here is legal advice, and every place the law decides something is marked **[counsel]**. Until the people in "Who is on call" are named, there is no one to follow this, so name them first.

## What the platform does by itself

The two reasons are named on every door: the report button on a post, comment, reel, message, group, stream or listing (the report dialog), the Safety Center's report form, and the public form at `/report`.

| What happens | When |
|---|---|
| The report is critical priority, with a reference (`RPT-...`) and a **24-hour** deadline, the clock the Online Safety Act sets for illegal content (not the 48-hour clock for harmful content). | Always, for `intimate_image` and `threat`, from any door. |
| An alert marked `[CRITICAL]` goes to the Trust & Safety mailbox (`TRUST_SAFETY_EMAIL`). It names ATHENA's own target: **a person opens it within 4 hours**. | Always. |
| A post, comment or reel is **hidden at once**, not deleted, and its author is told "something serious was reported" (never what, or by whom). | One report from a **signed-in** member, for an intimate image, a threat, child abuse material or terrorism. |
| An intimate image is queued as a referral to the **eSafety Commissioner** (Admin console, Authority referrals) and the referral desk is emailed. Nothing is sent to the Commissioner by itself. | Always, for `intimate_image`. |
| The report sits at the top of the queue, and the report deadline sweep lists a critical report that no person has claimed or decided after 4 hours, before its 24 hours run out. This is the backstop if the alert was missed; it also lists the reports that were hidden on the spot, which sit in review with no person behind them. | For a report from a **signed-in** member. A report from no account has no "claimed" state to check, so only the 24-hour sweep lists it, once it is late. |
| The reporter is shown, on the confirmation, the eSafety Commissioner, Policelink and 000. | Always, for these two reasons. |

What it does **not** do, on purpose:

- **It does not suspend or ban anyone.** One report can be wrong, and a woman can be reported by the person she is hiding from. Bans are a person's decision, below. This is recorded in the trust claims register, and the public guidelines say the same.
- **A report from no account does not hide anything on its own.** The public form is open to anyone, so it cannot be a way to take things down. It is critical, and the alert to the Trust & Safety mailbox goes out at once with the same 4-hour target; but the sweep that reminds the team after 4 hours covers only signed-in reports (above), so for these the alert is what holds the target.
- **It does not hide a direct message, a group or channel message, a profile or a story.** They have no hidden state to put them in. They are handled by a person, from the report, within the target. For a message, the report already holds a copy of it and the few before it (the reporter is told so in the dialog), because a message can be unsent or deleted. Removing a message deletes the row; the copy on the report is what is left.
- **It does not decide whether a threat is credible**, or send the police anything.

## Your first four hours

1. **Open the report** from the queue (Admin console, Moderation). It is at the top. Opening it is the target.
2. **Do not open, copy, forward or screenshot an intimate image** beyond what you need to decide. Do not email it, do not paste it into chat, do not save it. The report keeps the reference and, for a message, a copy of the words; for a post or reel the content itself stays where it is, hidden. If you need to look, look once, on the console.
3. **Is it what it says?** If it is: remove it (the decision **Remove**). That keeps the post hidden and tells the author, with a reference and the way to appeal. If it is not: **Dismiss**. Dismissing puts back what a report hid (a post, comment or reel), unless another report about it is still waiting, or a person has already decided against it on another report (a removal, a warning, a ban), or it was down for some other reason. So when two women report the same image, **Remove** it on one and **Dismiss** the other as a duplicate in either order: the removal stands.
4. **Is anyone in danger now?** See "A threat" below. If someone may be, that comes before everything else on this page.
5. **Decide about the person who posted it.** Suspend or ban from the same screen (the decisions **Suspend** and **Ban**). A ban locks the account, ends every session and socket at once, and bars the address from registering again; **a person confirms it, in the first hours, not the next week.** An appeal can reverse it. For an intimate image shared without consent, or a credible threat, the expected answer is a ban once you are satisfied.
6. **Tell the reporter** with a decision, as the console does. Do not say who the other person is, or what happened to their account beyond "we looked at it and took action".

## An intimate image shared without consent

- **The woman it is of is very often the only person who will report it.** Be quick and be kind. She may be frightened of what the person will do next. She can report from the report button, from the Safety Center or from `/report` (with no account, using an email address if she wants updates).
- **Tell her about the eSafety Commissioner.** It can require the websites and apps an image is on to take it down (including services ATHENA has no say over, though it cannot promise every one will comply) and has powers against the person who shared it. Her confirmation screen already says so; say it again in the reply. Her report to eSafety is hers to make; staff can lodge one on her behalf only if she asks and consents.
- **The referral on the Authority referrals screen is yours to work.** It starts as `reported` and is moved on when something real has happened: you have lodged it with the Commissioner (put the Commissioner's reference on it and mark it acknowledged), or the Commissioner has told you it is closed (resolved). If a report turns out to be nothing, resolve it with a note saying so; do not leave it open.
- **If the eSafety Commissioner sends ATHENA a removal notice** (Terms 12A.2 says we comply within the time the notice specifies): the notice names the content and the time. **[counsel: confirm the time the Act allows, and who at ATHENA is the person served.]** Remove what it names inside that time, keep the notice, and record the reference on the report. If you cannot reach whoever is named in "Who is on call", the owner is the fallback.
- **Keep what the Commissioner or the police will ask for.** The hidden content (never delete it while the report is open), the report, the reference, the dates and times, the account it came from, and the moderation log on the report. Do not collect more than that, and do not keep it anywhere but the platform.
- **Where it came from may not be ATHENA.** If she says the image is somewhere else, tell her to report it there and to eSafety. We can only act on what is on ATHENA.

## A threat

- **If anyone may be in danger right now, call 000.** ATHENA does not know where a member is; give the police what is known (the account, what was said, when). The Privacy Act allows disclosing personal information to prevent a serious threat to a person's life, health or safety. **[counsel: confirm the position, and how to record each disclosure.]** Then write down what you told them, to whom, when.
- **A threat that is not happening now:** decide whether it is credible (is it specific? aimed at a person? does the account have a history of it? is the person it is aimed at already on ATHENA?). If it is, tell the reporter she can report it to the police (Policelink 131 444 in Queensland, 000 if in danger), offer to give her the reference to quote, and keep the evidence as above. If the person it threatens is a member, consider whether to tell her yourself; she may be being threatened by someone she has blocked.
- **ATHENA does not hand a member's details to police or anyone else on request.** The police ask for them with a notice or a warrant, or under the serious-threat rule above. A request goes to the owner and to counsel before anything leaves the platform. **[counsel: approve this step and the way each request is logged.]**
- **A threat reported by the person it is aimed at does not mean she is the risk.** The report counts on the reported member's safety score once, however many times she is reported (and not at all for a report about someone who may hurt herself), and unchecked reports and blocks cannot, between them, take the score low enough to raise the staff flag. Only a report a person has upheld counts in full.
